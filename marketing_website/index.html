The Problem

You contracted millions of lives. A fraction of them know it.

Digital health vendors get paid on utilization, and increasingly on shared savings tied to outcomes. But the gap between covered lives and actively engaged members is enormous, and closing it is one of the hardest operational problems in the industry.

01

Outreach is blunt

Most outreach is templated, single channel, and disconnected from what a member is actually doing. It doesn't reach people at the moment they're most likely to act.

02

Data is scattered

Engagement data, claims, outreach history, demographics. It all lives in different systems. By the time an insight surfaces, the moment to act on it has passed.

03

No analytics foundation

Nobody builds good engagement analytics from the ground up. The only time anyone looks is when a big enterprise QBR is coming and everyone needs answers fast.

04

Decisions are guesswork

Messaging decisions are often made by people in a room deciding what will resonate with a population they don't look like or live like. There's no data backing it up.
